2. What the App Does

The App is a piano chord learning tool for iPad. It names and displays chords on left-hand and right-hand piano keyboards, plays them back, accompanies you with drums, and teaches chords and voicings through lessons.

3. Plans

The App has a Free plan and three paid plans: Bronze, Silver and Gold. Here is what each plan unlocks, at a high level. The plans screen in the App always shows the current, detailed list for each plan, and that list controls if it differs from this summary.

  • Free: identify and display any chord you type. Free does not include playback.
  • Bronze: single-chord playback (Play One), inversions, the basic chord library, Tips, receiving songs from friends, and 1 profile.
  • Silver: everything in Bronze, plus full-progression playback with drums, tempo and time-signature control; 7ths, 9ths and slash chords; unlimited song saving; the Performance Sheet with set lists; Grand Staff notation and guitar chord charts; transposed playback; exporting songs to friends; and up to 3 profiles.
  • Gold: everything in Silver, plus the full advanced chord library, lessons and chord resolutions, more set lists, up to 5 profiles, and new features as we add them.
  • Gold Lifetime: Gold access with a single, one-time payment, for as long as we offer and support the App. "Lifetime" means the lifetime of the App, not of the purchaser.

We may add, change or improve features over time.
