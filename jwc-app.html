How it works

Three moves. That's the whole method.

01 · Chart

Map the chords

Build a progression and see every chord laid out as a clear, dynamic diagram — no sheet music required.

02 · Invert

Smoothly invert

Reshape each chord so your hands glide from one to the next. Smooth voice leading, made visual.

03 · Jam Along

Play with the band

Hit play and jam along with the simulated backing band. Real music, real time, from the very first session.
